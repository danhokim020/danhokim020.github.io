  1. 한국문헌정보학회지
    An Interpretive Phenomenological Approach to Library Place-Shaping and Perception of Libraries: Focusing on Student Users of Academic Libraries
    Danho Kim and G. Kim
    Journal of the Korean Society for Library and Information Science (한국문헌정보학회지), 2026
    Abs

    This study explores how place-shaping occurs and how perceptions of academic libraries are constructed, focusing on student users of academic libraries. To this end, in-depth interviews were conducted with ten student users of academic libraries, and the data are analyzed using Interpretative Phenomenological Analysis (IPA). The results are interpreted using Self-Determination Theory (SDT), indicating that individual users select and construct specific places to fulfill psychological needs. Furthermore, it is suggested that different contextual factors shape place by influencing the process of need fulfillment, and that multiple place experiences are integrated into a holistic perception of libraries.
